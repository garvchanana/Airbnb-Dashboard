# Airbnb Performance Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze Airbnb listings, reviews, pricing behavior, and customer engagement patterns.

The dashboard evaluates:
- Platform growth trends
- City-wise market distribution
- Pricing insights
- Customer ratings & reviews
- Seasonal travel demand
- Host trust & verification indicators

---

# 📊 Dashboard Pages

## 1️⃣ Overview Dashboard

Analyzes Airbnb platform expansion and listing growth trends across property categories.

### Key Metrics
- 279K+ Listings
- 182K+ Hosts
- 144 Property Types
- 5.3M+ Reviews
- Coverage across 10 Cities

### Insights
- Peak listing growth observed around 2015.
- Listing activity gradually declined after 2019 during the COVID-19 period.
- Entire Place properties dominated the platform.

![Overview Dashboard](Images/overview.png)

---

## 2️⃣ Market Share & Ratings Analysis

Focuses on city-level market performance and customer satisfaction metrics.

### Analysis Included
- Market share distribution by city
- Property-type pricing comparison
- Ratings heatmap across cities
- Customer satisfaction evaluation

### Key Insights
- Paris, New York City, and Sydney contributed nearly half of total listings and reviews.
- Mexico City and Rio achieved the strongest overall customer ratings.
- Cleanliness and value-for-money metrics showed relatively lower scores across multiple cities.

![Ratings Dashboard](Images/ratings.png)

---

## 3️⃣ Reviews & Trust Analysis

Examines reviewer behavior, seasonal demand, and platform trust indicators.

### Analysis Included
- Review frequency distribution
- Monthly seasonality patterns
- Identity verification analysis
- Host trust indicators

### Key Insights
- Most customers reviewed properties only once.
- European summer months showed the highest review activity.
- More than two-thirds of hosts were identity verified.

![Reviews Dashboard](Images/reviews.png)
